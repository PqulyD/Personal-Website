Volatility Forecasting and Iron Condor Maker

A financial analytics project focusing on volatility forecasting for stocks and the creation of iron condor options strategies.

Volatility is one of the most critical parameters in options trading and financial risk management. This project combines volatility forecasting techniques with automated iron condor strategy generation to create a comprehensive tool for options traders. Iron condors are advanced options strategies that profit from low volatility environments by selling both call and put spreads on the same underlying stock.

The project builds a predictive model to forecast future volatility based on historical price action, technical indicators, and market microstructure features. By combining accurate volatility predictions with iron condor construction algorithms, traders can identify optimal entry and exit points, manage risk more effectively, and maximize probability of profit. The approach demonstrates how machine learning can bridge the gap between theoretical options pricing and practical trading strategy execution.

Project Overview

This project consists of two interconnected components:

1. Volatility Forecasting Model

The forecasting engine uses time series analysis and machine learning to predict implied and realized volatility. Key features include:

  • Historical volatility calculations using rolling windows
  • Technical indicators (Bollinger Bands, ATR, etc.) for trend and volatility detection
  • Machine learning models trained on lagged volatility features
  • Clustering analysis to identify regime changes in market conditions

2. Iron Condor Strategy Generator

Once volatility is forecasted, the system automatically constructs iron condor positions optimized for the current market regime:

  • Automatic strike selection based on delta targets and probability of profit
  • Spread width optimization to balance risk and reward
  • Risk assessment with maximum loss calculations
  • Trade management rules for profit-taking and stop-loss exits

Strategy Recommendations

🎯 When to Deploy: Iron condors are most effective when forecasted volatility is low and expected to remain subdued. The RandomForest model indicates low future volatility, making this an ideal entry point for the strategy. The wide profit zone (15,958 to 16,532) relative to current price increases probability of success.

⏰ Time Decay Advantage: With only 5 days to expiration, theta decay works in your favor. Each passing day erodes the value of the short options, increasing profit as time passes. This effect accelerates in the final 2 days before expiration.

🛡️ Risk Management: Set a stop-loss at 50% of max loss ($941) to exit early if adverse price movement occurs. Consider closing the position at 50-75% of max profit to lock in gains before expiration day.
