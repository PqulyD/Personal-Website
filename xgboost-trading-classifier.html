XGBoost News-Driven Stock Movement Classifier

Using ensemble machine learning to predict large stock price movements and optimize trading signals through advanced gradient boosting techniques.

This project harnesses the power of XGBoost and gradient boosting algorithms to classify stock price movements based on market features and news sentiment. By analyzing historical price behavior, volatility, and news impact, the model predicts whether a stock will experience significant price swings (>2% absolute movement). This is crucial for traders and portfolio managers seeking to navigate high-volatility trading opportunities and manage risk exposure.

The approach combines two top-tier machine learning algorithms and optimizes hyperparameters through exhaustive grid search, providing a data-driven trading decision support system. The goal is to identify trading opportunities with high probability of success while maintaining robust risk management protocols.

🎯 Trading Signal Framework

The classifier predicts whether a stock will experience significant movement (|return| > 2%) in the next trading period. This information serves as a powerful signal for trading decisions:

✓ Buy Signal (High Movement Predicted)

When the model predicts high volatility/movement:

  • Consider entering volatility-based positions (straddles, strangles)
  • Increase position sizing for hedged strategies
  • Monitor news and market sentiment closely
  • Set wider stop-losses to account for expected volatility

⊘ Hold Signal (Low Movement Predicted)

When the model predicts stable price action:

  • Deploy income strategies (covered calls, cash-secured puts)
  • Execute mean-reversion trades with tight stop-losses
  • Avoid high-volatility position structures
  • Focus on probability-of-profit over payoff ratios

⚙️ Hyperparameter Optimization

GridSearchCV was employed to systematically search the hyperparameter space and identify the optimal configuration:

Search Space:
  • n_estimators: [100, 200, 500] - Number of boosting rounds
  • max_depth: [3, 5, 7] - Tree complexity control
  • learning_rate: [0.01, 0.05, 0.1] - Shrinkage parameter for regularization
Best Parameters Found:
  • n_estimators: 500 - More boosting rounds captures complex patterns
  • max_depth: 5 - Allows deeper trees for better feature interactions
  • learning_rate: 0.01 - Conservative learning prevents overfitting
  • Best CV Accuracy: 91.7% - Excellent generalization

💡 Key Insights

🎯 XGBoost Outperforms: XGBoost achieved 89.2% accuracy compared to standard gradient boosting's 87.4%, demonstrating the value of optimized implementations and regularization techniques. Further tuning pushed this to 91.7%.

📈 Hyperparameter Impact: The optimal configuration (n_estimators=500, max_depth=5, lr=0.01) balances model complexity with generalization. Conservative learning rates prevent overfitting in smaller datasets.

🔍 Class Imbalance Handling: With 68% normal movements and 32% large movements, the model learned to balance sensitivity and specificity for actionable trading signals.

💰 Trading Application: High accuracy predictions enable traders to make informed decisions about position sizing, strategy selection, and risk management based on expected market volatility.
