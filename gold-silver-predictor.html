Gold, Silver, and GSR Random Forest Price Predictor With News

Project Overview

This financial analytics project leverages machine learning to predict precious metal prices by analyzing geopolitical events, market sentiment, and historical price data. The model focuses on gold, silver, and the gold-to-silver ratio (GSR) using Random Forest regression algorithms.

By incorporating news sentiment analysis and geopolitical factors, the system aims to identify price movements driven by global events, making it a tool for understanding how external news impacts commodity markets.

Key Features

  • Multi-Commodity Prediction: Predicts prices for gold, silver, and the GSR simultaneously
  • News Integration: Incorporates news sentiment and geopolitical events as features
  • Random Forest Modeling: Uses ensemble learning for robust price predictions
  • Feature Engineering: Develops meaningful features from raw market and news data
  • Model Evaluation: Comprehensive metrics including MAE, RMSE, and R² scores

Methodology

Data Sources: Historical commodity price data combined with geopolitical risk data and news indicators

Feature Engineering:

  • Geopolitical Risk: GPRD (Geopolitical Risk Index), GPRD_ACT (Actual Events), and GPRD_THREAT (Threat Level)
  • Lag Features: 1-day and 2-day lagged values for both gold and silver prices to capture temporal dependencies
  • Temporal Features: Month, day of week, and day of year to capture seasonal and cyclical patterns

Model Architecture:

  • Random Forest Regressor with 1,000 decision trees
  • Maximum depth: 12 levels
  • Minimum samples split: 25
  • Minimum samples leaf: 2
  • Max features: sqrt (square root of total features)

Multi-Output Regression: The model simultaneously predicts three target variables:

  • Gold Price
  • Silver Price
  • Gold-to-Silver Ratio (GSR)

Train-Test Split: 80-20 split with stratified sampling to ensure representative distribution across train and test sets.

Model Performance

The Random Forest model demonstrates strong predictive performance across all three targets:

  • Model R² Score: Comprehensive accuracy measurement across all prediction targets
  • Mean Absolute Error (MAE):
    • GOLD_PRICE: Measured in USD per troy ounce
    • SILVER_PRICE: Measured in USD per troy ounce
    • GSR (Gold-to-Silver Ratio): Ratio metric for relative precious metal valuation

The model captures complex non-linear relationships between geopolitical events and commodity prices, providing accurate predictions even with significant market volatility.

Visualization & Analysis

The project includes comprehensive visualization comparing actual vs. predicted values across all three targets:

  • Scatter Plots: Show the alignment between predicted and actual prices with reference lines indicating perfect predictions
  • Actual vs. Predicted: Clear visualization of model accuracy and any systematic biases
  • Multi-Output Analysis: Simultaneous evaluation of gold prices, silver prices, and their ratio

Insights & Outcomes

This project demonstrates how geopolitical risk indices and historical price patterns can be quantified and integrated into a machine learning framework to improve commodity price predictions. The Random Forest model successfully captures non-linear relationships between global events and precious metal prices, providing a data-driven approach to understanding market movements.

Key Insights:

  • Geopolitical risk events have measurable impacts on gold and silver prices, captured through the GPRD indicators
  • Historical price patterns (lag features) are strong predictors of future short-term movements
  • The Gold-to-Silver ratio moves independently of absolute prices, reflecting market sentiment shifts
  • The multi-output approach allows simultaneous prediction of correlated but distinct commodity behaviors

Applications: This model can be used for portfolio hedging decisions, identifying optimal entry/exit points for precious metal investments, and understanding how geopolitical developments propagate through commodity markets.
