Gold, Silver, and GSR Random Forest Price Predictor

View Code

This financial analytics project leverages a Random Forest regressor to predict precious metal prices by analyzing geopolitical risk indices and historical price patterns. The model simultaneously predicts three correlated but distinct targets: gold prices, silver prices, and the gold-to-silver ratio (GSR). By integrating geopolitical risk indicators with temporal market data, the system demonstrates how global events propagate through commodity markets in measurable ways.

The dataset combines historical commodity prices with geopolitical risk data over multiple years. Feature engineering includes the Geopolitical Risk Index (GPRD) with actual events and threat level components, lagged price values from the previous 1 and 2 days to capture short-term momentum, and temporal features (month, day of week, day of year) to model seasonal and cyclical patterns. This multi-faceted feature set allows the model to capture both the immediate impact of geopolitical shocks and the longer-term cyclical behavior of precious metal markets.

The Random Forest model is trained with 1,000 decision trees, each with a maximum depth of 12 levels and minimum samples per leaf of 2. The model uses square-root feature selection and is evaluated on a held-out test set (20% of data) using Mean Absolute Error (MAE) for each target and overall R² scoring. Actual vs. predicted scatter plots with reference lines provide clear visualization of model accuracy across all three prediction targets. The results demonstrate that the model captures non-linear relationships between geopolitical events and precious metal prices, providing a data-driven approach to understanding commodity market movements.

Model Architecture & Features

  • Random Forest Regressor: 1,000 trees with max depth of 12 and minimum samples split of 25
  • Geopolitical Features: GPRD index, actual events count, and threat level ratings
  • Temporal Features: 1-day and 2-day lagged gold/silver prices; month, day of week, day of year
  • Multi-Output Regression: Simultaneous prediction of gold price, silver price, and GSR
  • Train-Test Split: 80-20 split to ensure robust performance evaluation

Key Insights

  • Geopolitical risk events have measurable impacts on gold and silver prices captured through GPRD indicators
  • Historical price lag features are strong predictors of future short-term commodity movements
  • The Gold-to-Silver ratio moves independently of absolute prices, reflecting distinct market sentiment shifts
  • Multi-output regression allows simultaneous prediction of highly correlated but structurally different market behaviors
  • The model's performance demonstrates that machine learning can extract actionable patterns from the intersection of global events and commodity markets

Applications

This model can be used for multiple real-world applications including portfolio hedging decisions, identifying optimal entry and exit points for precious metal investments, risk assessment based on geopolitical developments, and understanding how global events propagate through commodity markets. Investors and financial analysts can use the price predictions to inform strategic trading decisions and hedge positions against geopolitical uncertainty.
